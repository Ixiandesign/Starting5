## Politicians

Scoped to **past U.S. presidents only**, reaffirming the same rationale the pre-wayfinder Phase-1
plan already landed on (git history, commit `3b45274`): sidesteps current-politics controversy
entirely. List stops at Obama (left office 2017) — a deliberately conservative cutoff, not just
"not currently serving." **Flagged below**: extending closer to the present is the owner's call.

1. **George Washington** — First president, set the two-term precedent by choice.
2. **John Adams** — Founding Father, one term, later reconciled with rival Jefferson.
3. **Thomas Jefferson** — Declaration author, doubled the country's size with Louisiana.
4. **James Madison** — "Father of the Constitution," led through the War of 1812.
5. **James Monroe** — Monroe Doctrine, the "Era of Good Feelings."
6. **Andrew Jackson** — Populist general whose presidency reshaped the party system.
7. **Abraham Lincoln** — Held the Union together through its bloodiest war.
8. **Ulysses S. Grant** — Union general who became president after winning the war.
9. **William Howard Taft** — Only person to serve as both president and Chief Justice.
10. **Theodore Roosevelt** — Trust-buster and conservationist, youngest president ever.
11. **Woodrow Wilson** — Led the U.S. through World War I, pushed the League of Nations.
12. **Franklin D. Roosevelt** — Four terms, the New Deal, and World War II.
13. **Harry S. Truman** — Ended WWII, launched the Marshall Plan and NATO.
14. **Dwight D. Eisenhower** — Supreme Allied Commander turned interstate-highway president.
15. **John F. Kennedy** — Cuban Missile Crisis, the moon-shot speech, a presidency cut short.
16. **Lyndon B. Johnson** — Civil Rights Act, Great Society, Vietnam escalation.
17. **Richard Nixon** — Opened relations with China, resigned over Watergate.
18. **Gerald Ford** — Took office after Nixon's resignation, never elected VP or president.
19. **Jimmy Carter** — Camp David Accords, a presidency later defined by his post-office work.
20. **Ronald Reagan** — Cold War hawk turned arms-reduction dealmaker.
21. **George H. W. Bush** — Gulf War commander-in-chief, one-term president.
22. **Bill Clinton** — Budget surpluses and impeachment in the same two terms.
23. **George W. Bush** — Presided through 9/11 and two long wars.
24. **Barack Obama** — First Black president, passed the Affordable Care Act.
